Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our placement programs and payment structure.

How does the milestone payment structure work?

Each package requires an upfront fee to initiate resume optimization and recruiter marketing. A second milestone payment is due only after you receive your designated interview . Once you are successfully placed, the remaining percentage (10%, 15%, or 12%) is payable in 3 easy installments starting from your first paycheck.

What does the Interview Guarantee cover?

We guarantee a set minimum count of direct interview based on your plan (Basic: 3 interviews, Standard: 5 interviews, Premium: 10 interviews). Our reverse recruiting team continues outreach until your guarantee is fulfilled.

Do you support candidates across non-IT and diverse industries?

Yes! Hyrex Placement supports job seekers across all major U.S. industries, including Business Administration, Healthcare, Operations, Finance, Marketing, Logistics, Management, and Engineering.

How many applications are submitted daily?

Depending on your chosen package, your personal associate recruiter submits up to 40–50 (Basic), 60–70 (Standard), or 70–90 (Premium) targeted job applications daily on your behalf.

How does the Refer & Earn $100 program work?

Simply refer any candidate looking for career placement in the U.S. Once they enroll with Hyrex, you receive a $100 cash referral bonus!

Terms & Conditions

Please review the official terms associated with our placement services.

  • All packages require the specified initial upfront payment to initiate resume rewriting and associate recruiter allocation.
  • The secondary milestone payment is due strictly upon achieving the interview counts outlined in your plan (1 interview for Basic, 2 interviews for Standard & Premium).

  • Technical & mock interview support sessions vary by plan (Standard: 3 sessions, Premium: At least 5 sessions) and are scheduled based on candidate availability.
